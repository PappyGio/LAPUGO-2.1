About LapuGo

Lakaw Ta, Go Beyond!

A Cebu-based travel concept focused on making island adventures easier to understand, compare and request.

Explore. Check. Choose. Then, let's go!

LapuGo Travel & Tours is a Cebu-based travel company created to make discovering the beauty of Cebu’s islands easier, more exciting, and more convenient.

We specialize in island-hopping experiences that bring travelers closer to crystal-clear waters, beautiful islands, marine adventures, and unforgettable moments.

Our website serves as your starting point for your next island adventure. Here, you can explore featured tour packages, view the complete itinerary, check available rates, and easily see what is included in each package.

Whether you are traveling with friends, family, a partner, or a small group, LapuGo allows you to discover a package that fits your budget, schedule, and preferred activities.
