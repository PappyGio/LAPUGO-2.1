TIKTOK
------
Video supplied by LapuGo:

The homepage uses TikTok's official Embed Player:
https://developers.tiktok.com/docs/en/embed-player

The player is configured with autoplay, muted audio and looping. JavaScript uses an
IntersectionObserver to request play when the player is visible and pause when it
leaves the viewport. Browser autoplay rules can still override automatic playback,
but the player remains interactive.
